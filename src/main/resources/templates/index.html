Welcome to the Forest Fire Prediction System

Utilize NASA's environmental data to analyze current conditions and historical patterns, generating predictive wildfire risk forecasts for specific locations through machine learning and environmental data analysis.

Earthdata Account

In order to proceed, a NASA Earthdata account is required to securely access data from AppEEARS. If you do not have an account, you can create one here.

Environmental Data Retrieval

Clicking the button below securely connects to NASA AppEEARS using your Earthdata account credentials to retrieve key environmental variables, such as land surface temperature and tree density, required for wildfire prediction. Your credentials are used only for this request and are not stored.

Processing Notice

Because extracting time-series satellite data from NASA’s servers can take some time, a background task will be created to handle the process. Once the data is ready, it will be used to generate the wildfire prediction, which may also take a short while to complete. The final results will be sent to the email address entered below.
