Welcome to the Forest Fire Prediction Portal

Generate localized wildfire risk and predictive forecasts for your selected coordinate point using historical and real-time NASA environmental data layers.

Predictive Data Retrieval

Clicking the button below securely passes your credentials to NASA AppEEARS to request the critical point-sample variables (such as fuel moisture, LST, and vegetation health indices) needed for the fire prediction model.

Processing Notice

Because extracting time-series satellite layers takes a few moments on NASA's servers, a background extraction task will be generated. The system will automatically feed this data into the predictive pipeline once complete.
